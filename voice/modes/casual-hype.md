# Mode: casual-hype

Short, punchy, "no way / this slaps / absolute W" register. The English equivalent of 개꿀/우와대박. Used when the substance is "we just shipped this wild thing" and the writer's affect is genuine excitement, not staged enthusiasm.

## Beats

1. **The reaction first**: emotional hit, then the thing. "no way this works" / "absolute W of a debugging session" / "wait this actually shipped".
2. **The thing**: what it is, in 1 sentence, with one specific anchor.
3. **(Optional)** one more line of why it's wild.
4. **End** — no CTA, no "agree?", no "what do you think".

## Required anchors

- One specific number / name / artifact. The hype only lands if there's a concrete thing being hyped.

## Length

- X: 1 tweet, often <200 chars.
- LinkedIn: rarely fits — but if used, ≤500 chars and skip if it'd feel forced for the audience.
- Reddit: only on casual subs (r/SideProject, r/programmerhumor, r/[topic]_memes). 50–150 words.

## Tonal vocabulary (allowed, ≤1 each)

- `wild`, `actually nuts`, `goated`, `cracked`, `slaps`, `absolute W`, `no way`, `lowkey`, `fr`, `legit`, `tbh`
- `game-changer` is permitted exactly once in this mode (banned in all other modes)

## Tonal vocabulary (banned even here)

- Everything from §1 of `ai-tells.md` is still banned.
- Forced "lol", "lmao", "ngl" if they don't sit naturally.
- More than 1 emoji per post.

## Anti-patterns

- Don't fake the affect. If the substance isn't actually exciting, switch to growth-story mode.
- Don't stack hype words. Pick one. "Absolute W" or "this slaps" or "no way" — not all three.
- Don't end on "what do you think?" — the post is a self-contained beat.
- Don't preface with explanation. Hype-first.

## Worked example (X, 180 chars)

> ok we just got the autoscaler down to 47% of last month's k8s bill and i'm not sure how. one of those "ship it before it un-ships itself" moments. config diff is 6 lines.

## Worked example (X, 110 chars)

> three weeks debugging the flakiest test of my life. fix was `scope="function"`. one word. absolute W.

## Worked example (Reddit r/SideProject, ~100 words)
